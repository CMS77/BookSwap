Safety Guidelines

Last updated: January 1, 2025

Arranging an Exchange

Use the built-in chat to coordinate the details of your exchange before meeting. Agree on a time, place, and method of delivery in advance. Never share sensitive personal information such as your home address in the chat — prefer public meeting locations.

Meeting in Person

When meeting another user to exchange a book, we recommend:

  • Choosing a public, well-lit location such as a library, café, or shopping centre
  • Letting someone you trust know where you are going
  • Meeting during daylight hours when possible
  • Bringing a friend if you feel more comfortable

Protecting Your Account

Never share your password with anyone, including other BookSwap users. Use a unique password that you don't use on other websites. Log out of your account when using shared or public devices.

Handling Books with Care

Treat borrowed books as if they were your own. Return them in the same condition you received them. If a book is accidentally damaged, communicate openly with the owner and try to reach a fair resolution.

Respectful Communication

Be polite and respectful in all your interactions. Do not use the chat to send offensive, threatening, or inappropriate messages. Users who engage in harassment may have their accounts suspended.

Reporting Issues

If you experience a problem with another user — such as a book not being returned or inappropriate behaviour — please contact us at safety@bookswap.example.com. We take all reports seriously.

Ratings and Trust

The rating system exists to help build a trustworthy community. Always leave honest ratings after completing an exchange — they help other users make informed decisions.
