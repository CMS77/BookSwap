Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name, username, and password. Optionally, you may also provide a profile photo, biography, and location. All messages sent through the platform are stored to enable the chat feature.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used exclusively to operate the BookSwap platform. This includes:

  • Authenticating your identity when you log in
  • Displaying your profile to other users
  • Facilitating book exchange requests and chat
  • Calculating your average rating based on completed exchanges

3. Information Visible to Other Users

Your username, name, profile photo, bio, location, and rating are visible to other registered users. Messages are only visible to the two participants of a swap request conversation.

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored in a secure database. Profile photos and book covers are stored as binary data in the database. We do not share your data with third parties.

5. Authentication

We use JSON Web Tokens (JWT) for authentication. Your token is stored locally in your browser and expires after 24 hours. Passwords are stored using secure one-way hashing (BCrypt) and cannot be recovered.

6. Your Rights

You may update your profile information at any time from your profile page. You may also delete books you have listed. If you wish to delete your account entirely, please contact us.

7. Cookies and Local Storage

BookSwap uses your browser's local storage to keep you logged in between sessions. No tracking cookies are used.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page.

9. Contact

For any privacy-related questions, please contact us at privacy@bookswap.example.com.
